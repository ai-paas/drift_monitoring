# Component Guide: Decision Or Summary Cards

Explain cards as context, not as graph evidence.

Use cards to identify:

- analyzed feature or chart purpose,
- event date or time,
- key score or p-value,
- sample count,
- summary metric,
- explicit decision label.

Do not let the card alone override graph/table evidence. If a card contains a decision, say that the card reports the decision and then connect it to visible evidence from other components.
