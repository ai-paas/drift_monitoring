# CHART-CTRL-003 Guide

This chart explains a multivariate MEWMA alarm where several metrics rise together.

Read in this order:

1. Summary card: MEWMA max, alarm time, and top contributing metric.
2. MEWMA statistic chart: statistic trend relative to UCL and alarm marker.
3. Contribution bars: metric-level contributions at the alarm point.
4. Small multiples: time-aligned movement of the individual metrics.
5. Notes/legend: use as context only.

The chart-level interpretation should connect UCL crossing, simultaneous metric movement, and the largest contribution. Contribution bars identify which metrics dominate the statistic; they do not prove the operational cause.

Do not treat one metric as the sole cause unless the chart data makes that explicit. Keep the conclusion at detector-evidence level.
