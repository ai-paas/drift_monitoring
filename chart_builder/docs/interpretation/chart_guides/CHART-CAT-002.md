# CHART-CAT-002 Guide

This chart explains categorical mix drift using reference/current proportions, absolute category deltas, and a Total Variation trend.

Read in this order:

1. Summary card: Total Variation, total absolute movement, and maximum signed delta.
2. Proportion grouped bars: categories where current proportion decreased or increased versus reference.
3. Delta ranking: largest absolute category movements and their signed direction.
4. Total Variation trend: movement over windows and whether it crosses or touches the management threshold.
5. Notes/legend: use as context only.

The chart-level interpretation should connect search proportion decrease, purchase/cart/return increases, total variation value, and the latest trend relative to the management threshold.

Do not treat Total Variation alone as a root cause. It measures probability mass movement across categories; segment, traffic source, campaign, collection, and label checks are follow-up evidence.
